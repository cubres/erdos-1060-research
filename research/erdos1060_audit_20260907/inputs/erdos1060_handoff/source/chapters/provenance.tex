\section{Preservation and edits}
The original source files and ZIP packets are retained unchanged. The consolidated
LaTeX renumbers labels, resolves local command conflicts, groups related proofs,
and adds dependency links and scope notes. Redundant status reports and repeated
search narratives were removed. The companion JSON catalogue preserves exact
large integers rather than relying on line-wrapped PDF extraction.

The handoff archive includes SHA-256 hashes for all distributed files except the
manifest itself. These detect transfer changes; they do not certify mathematical
truth. No model weights, private credentials, or font files are included.

\section{Final record boundary}
This compilation is a handoff of the supplied research, not an additional claim
of a new bound or a completed solution. Its central unresolved statement is still

sufficient criterion in the dossier remains conditional on proving its explicitly

conversation, but should not convert the working theorems into externally
